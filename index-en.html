YJing

The core application for exploring and managing personal photos and videos on visionOS. Relive every touching moment in a completely new spatial dimension.

Go to App Store Watch Video

Core Features

Immersive Photo Wall

Images surround you, interact naturally with gestures, browse in groups, rotate freely, offering an unprecedented visual feast.

Local Media Management

All spatial media files are securely stored on your local device, supporting offline access and management, keeping data in your control.

Convenient Import

Quickly import spatial photos and videos from the system "Photos" app, easily syncing your precious memories.

Multi-level Directory Management

Freely create, name, and move multi-level directories to efficiently categorize and find your photo and video content.

Waterfall Favorites

One-click favorite content, displayed in an immersive waterfall layout with adjustable thumbnail sizes for easier browsing.

Privacy Security Guard

Supports setting an access password and enabling Optic ID, fully protecting your personal privacy for secure storage of beautiful memories.

Feature Details

Photo Wall Mode: A New Spatial Interaction Experience

  • Click the red button on the toolbar to instantly enter the immersive photo wall space.
  • Supports photo and video display; single-click to zoom, double-click to play/pause video.
  • Browse by group, set the number per group and random display, switch freely.
  • Photo wall and sky sphere support manual drag rotation or auto-rotation.
  • Click to enlarge photos during rotation, configurable whether to stop rotation when enlarged.

Powerful Media File Management

  • Directory Management: Create, rename, copy, move, and delete multi-level directories.
  • Import Function: Quickly import spatial media from the Photos app.
  • File Operations: Supports moving, copying, renaming, and deleting files and directories.

Keep your media library organized and easily find content.

Favorites Management & Preview

  • One-click favorite photos and videos for centralized management.
  • Favorites use a waterfall layout for a great visual effect, supporting drag-to-adjust thumbnail size.
  • Compatible with all spatial media formats within the Photos app.
  • Use arrow keys during preview to smoothly switch between previous/next files.
